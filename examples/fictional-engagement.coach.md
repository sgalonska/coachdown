- would like to keep doing the shop windows

### Decisions
- Mara talks to her father before the next Monday
- the shift leads plan May as well

  - M: he is more ready to let go than I am to take over

## 2026-03-10 · Session 2
time: 14:00–15:00

### Since last time

> **Mara's notes before the session**
> I opened the new shop. Everything went fine, but I was there from 4 am to 9 pm and checked every tray. On Sunday evening I cried in the car and I don't really know why.

### Discussion
- What happened on Sunday evening?
  - tired, but also "empty"
  - "Nobody would have noticed if I had stayed at home"
- What does that thought mean to you?
  - fear of not being needed
  - relief, a little
  - I check everything because I don't trust myself, not because I don't trust them!!

### J: Scaling
- How confident are you that the shops run without you – on a scale from 1 to 10?
  - M: 4 last month, 6 today
- What would make it a 7?
  - M: one weekend off where nobody calls me

### Experiments
- [ ] Let the shift leads plan the April schedule on their own
