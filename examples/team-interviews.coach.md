> Fictional example. Rosa runs the kitchen of a restaurant with 15 staff. With her consent and theirs, the coach interviews two team members, then discusses the results with Rosa. Interview sessions are part of the same log, with `type: interview` and their own participants. In an interview, unmarked lines are the interviewee's.

# Rosa & Jonas – Coaching Log

## Agreements

- interviews with team members only with their consent
- Rosa sees a summary, never who said what word for word
- interview notes stay in this log and are not shared with the restaurant owner

---

## 2026-09-15 · Session 2
time: 15:00–16:00

### Discussion
- J: summary of both interviews
  - both value her standards
  - both feel they learn by being corrected, not by being shown
  - both would like a short daily briefing before service
- first reaction: "That's not fair, I explain things all the time"
- When do you explain – before or after something went wrong?
  - mostly after
  - I teach by correcting, the way I was taught!!
### Decisions
- = a ten-minute briefing at 17:00 before every service

### Experiments
- [ ] Run the briefing for two weeks; each day one dish explained before service

---

## 2026-09-08 · Interview Kim
type: interview · participants: J, K

- second year in the kitchen, works on the pass
- What do you need to get better faster?
  - "Someone showing me once instead of correcting me ten times"
- What does Rosa do that helps you?
  - she never lets a bad plate go out – "I trust her taste completely"

---

## 2026-09-08 · Interview Aaron
type: interview · participants: J, A

- sous-chef for four years
- What would you change in the kitchen tomorrow?
  - a short briefing before service, the way it was under the previous chef
  - J: Have you suggested it to Rosa?
    - "Once. She said there is no time."
- What should Rosa never change?
  - her standards

## 2026-09-01 · Session 1
type: intake

### Contracting
- wants the team to "think for themselves"
- How will you notice that they do?
  - fewer questions during service
  - someone fixes a problem before she sees it
- = Jonas interviews two team members next week, with their consent
