# Lea, Tom & Jonas – Coaching Log

## Goals

- Agree on who decides what in the firm
- Stop arguing about projects in front of the team

## Context

- founded the firm together eight years ago
- Lea leads design, Tom leads clients and finances
- two senior architects have left in the last year

---

## 2026-06-02 · Session 3
time: 09:00–10:30 · participants: J, L, T

### Since last time
- L: held the design review without Tom for the first time
- T: found it hard not to be there
  - T: "I read the minutes twice"

### Review
- [x] Write down separately which decisions each of us wants to own
  - learned: the lists overlapped in only three points
- [ ] Tell the team about the new review rule
  - not done – they wanted to agree first on how to say it

### Discussion
- the three overlapping points: hiring, fees for small projects, the office move
- How do you decide if you disagree on one of them?
  - L: we argue until one of us gives up
  - T: usually me, and then I'm annoyed for a week
  - ! T: giving in is not the same as agreeing
- J: You both describe the same pattern from opposite sides
  - L: I didn't know he felt like that

### Perspective: the senior architects who left
- as told by Lea and Tom from the exit talks
- "we never knew whose feedback counted"

### Decisions
- = disagreements on the three shared points go to a fixed weekly 30-minute slot, not to the studio floor
- = hiring decisions need both; fees and the office move are Tom's call after hearing Lea

### Experiments
- [ ] Use the weekly slot twice and note what was decided
- [ ] Tell the team about the new review rule together on Monday
